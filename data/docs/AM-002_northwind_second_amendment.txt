SECOND AMENDMENT TO REVOLVING CREDIT AGREEMENT

dated as of May 1, 2026

NORTHWIND LOGISTICS, INC., HARBORLINE BANK, N.A., as Administrative Agent, and the Lenders party hereto agree as follows with respect to the Revolving Credit Agreement dated as of March 15, 2024 (as amended by the First Amendment, the "Credit Agreement"):

1. Extension. The definition of "Maturity Date" is amended by replacing "June 30, 2029" with "June 30, 2030".

2. Leverage. Section 7.11(a) is amended and restated to provide that the Consolidated Total Leverage Ratio as of the last day of any fiscal quarter shall not be greater than 3.75:1.00.

3. No other changes. All other terms of the Credit Agreement are ratified and confirmed.

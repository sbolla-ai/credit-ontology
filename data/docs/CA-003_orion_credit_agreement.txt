CREDIT AGREEMENT

Dated as of January 10, 2025

ORION FABRICATION CORP., as Borrower
SUMMIT RIDGE CAPITAL LLC, as Administrative Agent and sole Lender

1. THE FACILITY. The Lender hereby establishes in favor of the Borrower a revolving credit facility in a maximum principal amount of $75,000,000 (the "Revolving Commitment"), available until December 31, 2028 (the "Termination Date"), on which date all outstanding Revolving Loans shall be due and payable.

2. INTEREST. Revolving Loans shall accrue interest at Term SOFR plus 1.75% per annum.

3. FINANCIAL COVENANTS. So long as any Obligation remains outstanding:

(a) Leverage. The Borrower will maintain, as of the end of each fiscal quarter, a Total Leverage Ratio of not more than 3.00 to 1.00.

(b) Fixed Charge Coverage. The Borrower will maintain, as of the end of each fiscal quarter, a Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio of not less than 1.25 to 1.00.

(c) Capital Expenditures. Capital Expenditures shall not exceed $12,000,000 in any fiscal year.

4. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement and the other Loan Documents shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.

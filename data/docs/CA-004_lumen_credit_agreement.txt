Clause 2.1 The Facility. Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Lenders make available to the Company a multicurrency revolving credit facility in an aggregate amount equal to EUR 150,000,000.

Clause 8.1 Interest. The rate of interest on each Loan for each Interest Period is the percentage rate per annum which is the aggregate of the applicable Margin and EURIBOR. "Margin" means 2.00 per cent. per annum.

Clause 7.1 Repayment. Each Loan shall be repaid on the last day of its Interest Period, and all Loans shall be repaid in full on the Termination Date. "Termination Date" means 30 April 2030.

Clause 22.2 Financial condition. The Company shall ensure that:
(a) Leverage in respect of any Relevant Period shall not exceed 3.75:1; and
(b) Interest Cover in respect of any Relevant Period shall not be less than 3.50:1.

Clause 41 Governing law. This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it are governed by English law.
